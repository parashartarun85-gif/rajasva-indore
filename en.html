🌟 National Lok Adalat — Surcharge rebate on property tax & water tax

Under the National Lok Adalat, a rebate on the surcharge (penalty) charged on outstanding property tax and water tax is being offered. The facility is available for one day only — for the date and conditions, see the Corporation’s notice or ask at the zonal office.

Property tax — surcharge rebate by outstanding amount
Outstanding up to ₹ 50,000100% rebate
₹ 50,000 to ₹ 1 lakh50% rebate
Above ₹ 1 lakh25% rebate
Water tax — surcharge rebate by outstanding amount
Outstanding up to ₹ 10,000100% rebate
₹ 10,000 to ₹ 50,00075% rebate
Above ₹ 50,00050% rebate

What does “up to 100% rebate” mean? If the outstanding amount is small (property tax up to ₹ 50,000, water tax up to ₹ 10,000), the surcharge is waived in full (100%). The rebate percentage reduces as the outstanding amount grows.

Three ways to pay:

  1. Online — at www.mpenagarpalika.gov.in.
  2. At the Corporation’s zonal office.
  3. Through the Assistant Revenue Officer / Bill Collector using a POS machine.

How do I know my outstanding amount? You can get your dues through any of the three ways above and pay. For the online route keep your property tax (PT) account number or water tax account number ready.

What is surcharge? The extra charge added to the outstanding amount when tax is not paid on time.

Is the original tax waived? No. The rebate is only on the surcharge; the original tax amount remains payable.

What is a Lok Adalat? A mutual-settlement arrangement; on the fixed day you can pay and avail the rebate.

This page is for information only. Conditions and eligibility are as per the Corporation’s notice.

Before buying a property — check these three things
  1. Obtain an Encumbrance Certificate (भार-मुक्त प्रमाण पत्र). It shows whether the property carries any loan, mortgage or dispute. It is readily available from the Registrar Office in the Collectorate complex — view location →. A twelve-year search (बारह साला) is normally obtained.
  2. Match the area. The area written in the sale deed and the area recorded in the Corporation's property tax account must agree. A mismatch delays mutation and needs a separate correction application.
  3. Check for arrears. Confirm from the seller that no property tax, water charges or solid waste fee is outstanding.
After purchase, all arrears of the previous owner become payable by the buyer. A little care can save a large financial burden.
When to pay property tax — rebates and surcharge

The property tax bill is actually a combination of 6 different taxes and 1 service charge shown together, and the Property Tax itself is one of these 6 taxes. All components are calculated from the same property assessment and added into a single bill. So if the full amount is not paid in the current year, the 50% self-occupancy rebate is not available, and from the following year a surcharge (5%+5%+5% = 15%) is added to this same combined amount — so the total due keeps growing year after year. See the example below:

Why other items are affected: in this example, the Water User Charge is always exactly 10% of the Property Tax and the Drainage Tax is always exactly 20% of the Property Tax — this ratio holds in both years (compare ₹222/₹2,222 and ₹444/₹2,222 with ₹111/₹1,111 and ₹222/₹1,111). In other words, these two taxes are calculated directly from the Property Tax — the higher the Property Tax (when the rebate is not obtained), the higher these two will also be, in the same proportion. Education Cess, Samekit Tax and Urban Tax stayed the same in both years in this example, so these three do not appear to be directly linked to the Property Tax.
Overall effect: this is why the 50% rebate on Property Tax (₹2,222 → ₹1,111) also reduces the two linked taxes, bringing the total demand down from ₹4,842 to ₹3,398 — a direct saving of ₹1,444 for paying on time, with no surcharge at all. (Figures are illustrative and not linked to any specific property.)
  1. 50% rebate — granted by the State on self-occupied residential houses / flats / plots. This rebate is available only if the current financial year's property tax is paid regularly. If the tax is not paid, the rebate is not allowed.
  2. Additional 6.25% rebate (property tax) — on payment of property tax between 1 April and 30 June.
  3. Additional 6% rebate (water user charge) — on payment of the water user charge between 1 April and 30 June.
  4. Surcharge: 5% from 1 January, a further 5% from 1 February, and a further 5% from 1 March.
In short: the best time to pay property tax and other charges is 1 April to 30 June. If that is not possible, pay before 1 January — the surcharge rises every month thereafter. No rebate or surcharge applies to the solid waste management fee.

What the terms in your property tax ledger mean

These terms appear in the Detail Ledger Report downloaded from e-NagarPalika. General meanings are given below.

  1. Property Tax — the main tax levied on the Annual Letting Value (ALV) of the property.
  2. Education Cess — a cess for education, collected by the Corporation along with property tax.
  3. Samekit Kar (Consolidated Tax) — a consolidated tax collected together with property tax.
  4. Urban Tax — a tax levied for urban infrastructure and development works.
  5. Jal Upbhokta Prabhar (Water User Charge) — a user charge for the water supply service.
  6. Jal Nikas Kar (Drainage Tax) — a tax for the drainage / sewerage system.
  7. Sewa Kar (Service Charge) — a service fee for sanitation and other civic services.
  8. Penalty — a late fee / surcharge for not paying on time.
  9. Rebate — the amount of rebate granted by the State.
  10. CQ Bounce — a charge levied when a cheque given for payment is dishonoured.
  11. Form Fee — an administrative fee relating to the application / form.
This is for general understanding only. Please confirm the exact rate or method of calculation for any tax with the Revenue section of the office.
Rainwater harvesting — why Indore needs it
  1. Indore depends heavily on water pumped from the distant Narmada river. Bringing this water to the city takes enormous energy and expense.
  2. The groundwater level in the city keeps falling. Borewells are being dug deeper, and several areas face water scarcity in summer.
  3. A rainwater harvesting unit stops rainwater from running off and lets it soak into the ground, improving the water table nearby and keeping borewells working longer.
  4. For this reason a rainwater harvesting unit is required on buildings constructed on plots of 1500 sq ft or more (all multi-storey buildings fall here), and mutation is not completed without it.
This is not a formality — it directly affects the water level in your own locality. One unit can send thousands of litres into the ground in a single monsoon.

Online Payment & Complaint from home

How to pay online (e-Nagarpalika Citizen app)

Property tax, water charges and solid waste management (SWM) charges are all paid the same way. Get the app here: e-Nagarpalika Citizen app (Google Play Store) →

  1. Open the app. On the home screen choose by payment: Property Tax for property tax, Water Charges for water, Door To Door for the waste (SWM) charge.
  2. Select the corporation. The ULB 0152 – Indore Nagar Nigam is pre-selected. Enter your account number and tap Search —
    • Property tax: Property ID
    • Water: water account number (10 digits, starts with 5000; new accounts start with WC-2026)
    • SWM: D2D ID (10 digits, starts with 100; new accounts start with DC-2026)
  3. Tap your card. Name, address etc. are shown. Confirm it is your account.
  4. Check the bill. Arrear, current-year amount, Rebate and total payable are shown. Then tap Pay.
  5. Under Payment Mode choose Axis Bank, then tap Proceed.
  6. On the Axis Easypay page check the amount. Note down the URN shown there, then tick Terms and Conditions.
  7. Choose the payment method: Internet Banking, Cards, UPI or NEFT/RTGS. For UPI, enter your UPI ID, fill in the verification code and tap Complete Payment on Phone, then approve in your UPI app.
  8. Complete the payment within 3 minutes, otherwise the transaction is cancelled. After success a Download Receipt option appears — tap it to download your receipt.
Caution: Read the red disclaimer on the bill (the calculation follows system data; if a difference is found you may have to pay it later). Keep your UPI app and network ready because of the 3-minute limit. If money is debited but you get no receipt, contact your zone office quoting the URN.
How to pay online from the website (e-Nagarpalika portal)

Property tax, water charges and solid waste management (SWM) charges can all be paid this way from a mobile or computer browser. Website: enagarpalika.mp.gov.in →

  1. Open the website and go to Citizen Login. Under “Sign Up with” choose Mobile Number (or Samagra ID).
  2. In the Declaration box that appears, tick the consent box and tap Proceed.
  3. Enter your mobile number and tap Get Captcha. Type the letters shown into Enter CAPTCHA and tap Send OTP.
  4. Enter the 6-digit OTP received on your mobile and tap Verify & Login.
  5. In the left menu choose by payment: Property Tax, Water Charges or D2D (waste charge). Then tap Quick Pay.
  6. Under ULB select 0152 – Indore. Enter your account number and tap Search —
    • Property tax: Property ID
    • Water: water account number (10 digits, starts with 5000; new accounts start with WC-2026)
    • SWM: D2D ID (10 digits, starts with 100; new accounts start with DC-2026)
  7. Pick your row from the list. Match the Applicant, Owner and Property Address details, then check Tax Details: Arrear, current-year amount, Rebate and total.
  8. Tap Pay. In Pay Property Bill choose Axis Bank and tap Proceed.
  9. The rest is the same as paying through the app — check the amount on the Axis Easypay page, note the URN, tick the Terms, choose the payment method, finish the payment within 3 minutes and download the receipt. See the “e-Nagarpalika Citizen app” card above for details.
Good to know: Read the green disclaimer below the bill (the calculation follows system data; if a difference is found you may have to pay it later). The login page also offers a temporary Samagra ID (“Click Here”) if you do not have one, and a “Pay Via WhatsApp” option. If the captcha or OTP is wrong, try again. If money is debited but you get no receipt, contact your zone office quoting the URN.
How to file a correction complaint on the Indore 311 app

A property tax correction complaint can also be filed from home on the Indore 311 app. Follow the steps below.

  1. Open the Indore 311 app and log in with your mobile number. Tap “File Complaint” on the home screen.
  2. From the list, select “Property Tax Related Complaint”.
  3. Enter the property’s Khasra / Account / Plot number and your mobile number, then tap “Send OTP”.
  4. Enter the OTP received on your mobile and tap “Verify”.
  5. Fill in the requested details and the reason for correction, upload a PDF of the supporting document, then tap “Next”.
  6. Review the entered details once and tap “Submit”.
  7. The complaint will be filed successfully and a complaint number will be issued. Use this number to track status on the Indore 311 app. Example: CPC-2026-000000
Keep PDFs of the relevant property documents ready on your phone beforehand, so uploading is not a hassle.

Common reasons applications are returned — avoid these

Check these points before submitting. Most applications come back for these reasons, causing avoidable delay.

  1. Submitting an ordinary photograph instead of a geo-tagged one — the photograph must show latitude and longitude
  2. Property tax, water charges or solid waste fee remaining unpaid
  3. Not attaching a self-attested copy of the sale deed — the copy must carry the applicant's signature
  4. Missing Aadhaar cards or notarised affidavit of all heirs in inheritance cases
  5. No rainwater harvesting unit on a building on a plot of 1500 sq ft or more (including all multi-storey buildings) — mutation is not completed without it
  6. The area in the sale deed not matching the area recorded in the Corporation's property tax account — always compare the two before buying
  7. In inheritance by will / bequest, not attaching a copy of the will (original for verification), and documents not being self-attested
  8. In mutation of a multi-storey building, not attaching the identity proofs and consent of all unit owners
  9. For a new property tax account, not attaching the khasra / land-record copy and the self-assessment return

Mutation (नामांतरण) — purchase by registered sale deed

Documents to be submitted by the applicant

  1. Self-attested copy of the registered sale deed
  2. Photocopy of property tax receipt
  3. Photocopy of water charges receipt
  4. Solid waste management fee receipt
  5. Colour geo-tagged photograph of the building / flat / plot
  6. Aadhaar card of both buyer and seller
  7. Public notice published in a daily newspaper (विज्ञप्ति)
  8. Mutation fee receipt
Note: for a flat, a water charges receipt is not required. A solid waste fee receipt (the flat's own, or the building's bulk account receipt) is mandatory and the fee must be paid. For an empty plot, water and solid waste receipts are not required (attach them if an account is already open).
Mutation is completed only if a rainwater harvesting unit exists on a building constructed on a plot of 1500 sq ft or more (including multi-storey buildings).
Spot reports, notings, recommendations and other departmental papers are prepared by the office. The applicant does not have to bring these.

New water charges account

To get a new water connection (Water Supply Dept.)

A new water connection must be obtained from the Water Supply Department before a water charges account is opened. The applicant must provide:

  1. Proof of identity — Aadhaar card, voter ID or any other valid ID
  2. Proof of address — electricity bill, property tax receipt or any other valid address proof
  3. Affidavit — on a ₹100 non-judicial stamp paper, in the format prescribed by the Corporation (where applicable)
The affidavit must be in the prescribed format and on stamp paper. It is kept in the application file. You can view and print the format below (the format is in Hindi, as prescribed).

EWS Certificate 2 cases, with affidavit

The EWS certificate is valid from the date of issue until the end of that financial year (31 March); a new one must be made after 1 April. Read the information first, then open the option below that matches your case (self-owned / rented property).

What is EWS — eligibility, rules and process (read first)

What is EWS

  1. EWS stands for Economically Weaker Section.
  2. The Constitution (103rd Amendment) Act, 2019 inserted Articles 15(6) and 16(6). They allow reservation of up to 10% for economically weaker citizens in admission to educational institutions (except minority educational institutions) and in government jobs. This is in addition to the existing reservations.
  3. The benefit is for those who do not fall under Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes or the Other Backward Classes notified by the Government of Madhya Pradesh.
  4. The Amendment does not itself state income or property limits. These limits are fixed by government orders — for Madhya Pradesh, the General Administration Department order No. F 7-11/2019/A.P./Ek (June 2021).

Eligibility limits

CriterionMadhya Pradesh Govt. (Indore Municipal Corporation area)Central Government rules
Annual family incomeBelow Rs. 8 lakh (all sources — salary, agriculture, business etc.)Below Rs. 8 lakh (all sources)
Agricultural landNot eligible if more than 5 acres (land recorded in the khasra as barren, rocky or uncultivable continuously for 3 years is not counted)Not eligible if 5 acres or more
Residential house / flatIn a Municipal Corporation area, not eligible if more than 1200 sq. ft.Not eligible if a residential flat of 1000 sq. ft. or more
Residential plotNo separate limit is given in the orderNot eligible if 100 sq. yards or more in a notified municipality; 200 sq. yards or more in other areas
Note: the figures in the right-hand column are Central Government rules. The order gives separate criteria for Madhya Pradesh and for the Central Government.
  1. Family means: the applicant, his/her parents, spouse, and brothers/sisters and sons/daughters below 18 years of age.
  2. Property held by the family in different places / cities is added together.

Who issues the certificate and how long it is valid

  1. The EWS (income and asset) certificate is issued by the Collector’s office — by a Tehsildar or a higher officer (SDO). It is not issued by the Municipal Corporation.
  2. A complete application is disposed of within 15 working days.
  3. Validity: the certificate is valid from the date of issue until the end of that financial year (31 March). So a separate certificate is made for each financial year.
  4. When to get a new one: after 31 March you must get a new certificate for the new financial year (from 1 April). Example: a certificate made in November remains valid only up to the next 31 March. The certificate must be valid on the date of your job or admission application, so check its validity before you apply.
  5. If the income or property shown in the certificate goes down or up, you can apply with a copy of the old certificate and the required documents; the officer can cancel the old certificate and issue a new one.

Process in Indore

  1. Get a certificate from both the Indore Municipal Corporation and the Indore Development Authority that the applicant’s family does not hold property above the limit within the Corporation limits / Authority area.
  2. For the Municipal Corporation certificate, submit the affidavit at the Revenue Department of your zone office — documents and formats are in the options below (self-owned / rented property).
  3. Take both certificates to the Collector’s office and apply for the EWS certificate — online on the MP e-District portal, at a Lok Seva Kendra, or at the Collector’s office. You get an acknowledgement receipt on applying; keep it safe.
  4. In urban areas the Revenue Inspector does a field verification on site.
  5. A false affidavit or false declaration can lead to legal action under the Indian Penal Code and other provisions.

Documents for the application at the Collector’s office (as per the order)

  1. Samagra ID (of the family)
  2. If you hold property: registry / patta / nazool papers (land, plot, flat, house)
  3. If you own agricultural land: khasra and B-1
  4. If salaried, a trader, a professional etc.: Form 16 / ITR-V (previous financial year) / pay slip
  5. Self-declaration
Fee: there is no departmental fee. If you apply through a Lok Seva Kendra, the Kendra’s fixed service charge is paid separately to the operator.

Appeal if the application is rejected or not decided in time

  1. First appeal: to the Collector, within 30 days. The Collector decides within 30 working days.
  2. Second appeal: to the Divisional Commissioner, within 60 days.

Use of the certificate

  1. To claim the 10% reservation in admission to educational institutions and in government jobs.
This information is only for applicants residing within the Indore Municipal Corporation limits. Rules can change from time to time; the final decision rests with the Collector’s office.

  4. Rent agreement: if the applicant is a tenant
    • ☐ The rent agreement must be notarised on ₹500 stamp paper.
    • ☐ The rent agreement must be valid: its term must not have expired; no old / expired 11-month agreement.
  5. ID proof: ID of the licence applicant (Aadhaar card, PAN card or driving licence)

If the property is not in the applicant’s name

  1. If the property tax receipt is in the name of the mother, father, brother or husband, attach a notarised consent letter on Rs 50 stamp paper given by the owner shown on the receipt in favour of the licence applicant
  2. For a market shop rented out by the Nagar Nigam, the licence can be issued only in the name of the person mentioned in the market rent receipt

Additional documents by business / firm type

  1. Private Limited firm: company memorandum and an authority letter from the director in favour of the licence applicant
  2. Doctor / private clinic: doctor’s certificate issued by the Medical Council, or a copy of the degree, in the applicant’s name
  3. Beauty parlour / spa centre: police verification report from the police station area of the business premises, in the applicant’s name and showing the business address
  4. Insurance business: licence or approval report issued by IRDA in the company’s name
  5. Partnership firm: partnership deed
  6. Security services: current receipt from the IG office
  7. Money-lending licence: character certificate of the applicant issued by the SP office, plus an FD of Rs 50 lakh in the name of the Commissioner, Nagar Nigam, and a 3-year CA-certified balance sheet
  8. Finance company: licence issued by the RBI in the finance company’s name
  9. Hospital: certificate issued by the CHMO in the hospital’s name
  10. Chemist / medical store: Copy of the drug licence — retail: Form 20 / 21 / 20F, wholesale: Form 20B / 21B / 20G (as applicable); the firm name and shop address on it must match the application and it must be valid. Also the registration certificate of the qualified person (registered pharmacist / competent person). Selling Schedule X drugs needs a separate licence — Form 20F (retail) / Form 20G (wholesale).

Optional documents (if applicable)

  1. Memorandum
  2. Electricity bill
  3. Police verification
  4. Separate rent agreement

File formats & size

  1. jpg, png, svg or pdf — maximum 10 MB per file. Every document copy must be clear and readable

How to apply (on the e-Nagar Palika portal)

Steps for the citizen

  1. Log in to the e-Nagar Palika citizen portal with your Samagra ID or mobile number (OTP verification)
  2. Go to Trade License > New Trade License and select your Urban Local Body (e.g. Indore Nagar Nigam)
  3. Fill in applicant details (auto-fetched via Samagra ID / Property ID / Gumasta), firm details and address, and select the business items and quantities — the fee is calculated automatically
  4. Accept the terms and conditions and submit the form to get an Application Number (e.g. TLA-2026-006582)
  5. When you get the demand notification, pay the fee (cash or other modes) through the Cash Desk option on the portal
  6. Once the certificate is digitally signed after payment, search and download the Trade Licence Certificate in English from the portal
Common reasons a Trade Licence application is rejected — avoid these

Check these ten points before you submit. Most applications are rejected for these reasons, which delays your licence unnecessarily.

  1. Choosing the wrong code. Many businesses have an area category (for example “below 300 sq ft”). If your shop is larger than 300 sq ft and you pick the code for the smaller area, the application is rejected. Always pick the code that matches your actual area. Download code & rate list (PDF)
  2. Applying with a single code for mixed businesses. If you run more than one business, add the codes of all of them in one application. The fee is added up as per each code’s rate. To see the total fee first, add all your businesses in the Know trade licence fee card.
  3. Property tax account is residential. A trade is permitted only on a property in commercial use. If the account shows residential use, first get the use corrected in the property tax account, then apply for the licence. How to correct the use →
  4. Pending property tax or solid waste management (garbage) charges. Apply only after both are paid.
  5. Unclear copies of documents. Every document must be clear, complete and readable. Do not upload blurry photos or cropped pages.
  6. Shop in a mother’s, father’s, brother’s or husband’s property, with a rent agreement attached. In such a case, attach the notarised consent letter of the owner shown on the property tax receipt, on Rs 50 stamp paper, instead of a rent agreement.
  7. Not attaching the special documents required for your business type. Private Limited firm: company memorandum and the director’s authority letter; doctor / private clinic: Medical Council certificate or degree; beauty parlour / spa: police verification report; insurance: IRDA licence; partnership firm: partnership deed; security services: current IG office receipt; money-lending: SP office character certificate, Rs 50 lakh FD and 3-year CA-certified balance sheet; finance company: RBI licence; hospital: CHMO certificate; chemist / medical store: drug licence (Form 20 / 21 etc.) and the pharmacist’s registration certificate. See the “Documents required” box above for the full list.
  8. Chemist — shortcomings in the drug licence. The firm name or shop address on the licence differs from the application, the licence has expired, or it is for another shop / address. If the constitution of the firm changes (for example partnership to company), a fresh drug licence is needed — the old licence is treated as valid for three months only. The licence must be displayed prominently in the shop and any change of the qualified person (pharmacist) must be reported to the drug authority within one month.
  9. Applying in someone else’s name for a market shop rented out by the Nagar Nigam. The licence for such a shop can be issued only in the name of the person mentioned in the market rent receipt.
  10. Rent agreement not notarised on ₹500 stamp paper. If you operate from rented premises, the rent agreement must be notarised on ₹500 stamp paper.
  11. Invalid / expired rent agreement. The term of the rent agreement must not have expired. An old or expired 11-month agreement gets the application rejected; attach a valid agreement.
